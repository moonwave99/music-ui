# Music Notation

[ABC notation](https://abcnotation.com/) is a form of music notation that is easily parsable and editable by both people and machines.  
This library uses [abc.js](https://github.com/paulrosen/abcjs) to render ABC notation as music scores on screen.

The `@music-ui/abc` package provide various ways to render music notation:

- [the `initABCScore` function](/javascript/music-notation/basic-usage): the quickest way;
- [the `initABCScoreWithPlayer` function](/javascript/music-notation/usage-with-player): it adds playback functionality;
- [the `ABCScore` class](/javascript/music-notation/ABCScore): full control, but you have to wire it to your custom interface.
