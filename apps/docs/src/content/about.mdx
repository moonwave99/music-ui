# About

Over the years I developed various music representation tools for the web (e.g. [fretboard.js](https://moonwave99.github.io/fretboard.js)), that I used for tuition and self learning.

Finally I decided to gather and publish them as a monorepo in order to provide a shared build, release and type system.

## Rationale

The main reason behind the creation of the various keyboard, fretboard and score visualization libraries has been the **authoring of music lessons and exercises**.

## The author

My name is Diego, after a full-stack engineer career I am now a programming and music educator.

You can learn more about me [on my personal website](https://diegocaponera.com).
